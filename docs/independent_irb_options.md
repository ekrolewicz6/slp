# Independent IRB Feasibility

**Date:** 2026-04-30
**Status:** planning memo, not legal advice

## Bottom Line

Independent IRB review is feasible, but it should not be the default first move.

For the next few weeks, the best path is:

```text
existing data + SLP feedback + simulated recorder workflow
-> partner-lab conversation
-> decide independent IRB only if prospective collection is ready
```

The project can likely prepare for minimal-risk or expedited review, but audio
recordings are identifiable enough that we should not assume exemption without
formal determination.

## Why This Likely Needs Review

Prospective language-sample collection involves interaction with living people
and collection of voice/audio data. HHS/OHRP expedited categories explicitly
include voice/video/digital/image recordings made for research and research on
language/communication or interview-style behavior, when no more than minimal
risk and appropriate protections apply:
https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/guidance/categories-of-research-expedited-review-procedure-1998/index.html

Some survey/interview or benign behavioral-intervention work can be exempt, but
voice recordings, children, clinical populations, and potential identifiability
push this toward at least formal exempt determination or expedited review.

## Review Paths

| path | when to use | advantages | risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| no new human-subjects collection | current retrospective analyses | fastest, no new consent | cannot answer prospective treatment questions |
| SLP usability feedback only | reviewing mock reports/workflows with professionals | likely lower risk; fast | not patient data; cannot validate outcomes |
| partner-lab IRB | collection through university/clinic | credibility, recruitment, established policies | slower, dependent on partner |
| independent IRB | project-led prospective pilot outside academia | possible without university affiliation | cost, paperwork, need investigator responsibility |
| TalkBank-compatible deposit protocol | if recordings may enter TalkBank | aligns with Brian's infrastructure | consent language must be right upfront |

## Independent IRB Vendors To Consider

This list is for feasibility, not endorsement.

- Advarra: AAHRPP-accredited, OHRP/FDA registered, can serve as central or local
  IRB. Fee schedules are request-based.
  https://www.advarra.com/about/accreditation-and-compliance/
  https://www.advarra.com/solutions/review-services/institutional-review-board-resources/

  Connexus system and fee schedules are requested rather than fully public.

- BRANY: AAHRPP-accredited central/single IRB with SBER/social-behavioral
  review services.
  https://www.brany.com/
- Tempus IRB: publishes a pricing page and advertises rapid review; would need
  diligence on fit for child/clinical speech-language research.
  https://www.tempusirb.com/pricing

## Cost Expectation

Public fee information is inconsistent. Some institutional pages show fees in
the low thousands for IRB review; for example, UCI lists a 2026 IRB review fee
of $2,940 for certain industry-supported studies, while commercial IRBs often
require requesting a fee schedule:
https://research.uci.edu/human-research-protections/irb-fees/

Planning assumption:

```text
minimal/exempt determination: low thousands or less
full protocol review: several thousand dollars
amendments/continuing review/translations: additional cost
```

We should get actual quotes only after the pilot protocol is concrete.

## Likely Minimal-Risk Pilot Shape

The safest first prospective pilot:

- adults only, or SLP professional reviewers only;
- no patients/children initially;
- simulated or volunteer recordings;
- local-only recording;
- no diagnosis or treatment decisions;
- no raw media sharing outside the project;
- explicit spoken-PHI warning;
- opt-out and deletion process;
- report outputs labeled research-only.

The next level:

- adults with aphasia through a partner clinic or aphasia center;
- parent-permission child language samples through a partner lab;
- stuttering samples through a fluency clinic or research lab.

## Decision Rule

Use independent IRB only when all are true:

- we have a specific prospective question;
- we know the target population;
- recorder workflow has been tested on non-sensitive samples;
- consent and data retention are written;
- there is no partner-lab path likely to move faster;
- the result would materially advance the science beyond existing data.

Otherwise, keep building with existing data and SLP review.

## Recommendation

Do not start independent IRB paperwork today.

First complete:

1. refreshed TalkBank/FluencyBank data access;
2. Dryad EMT-SF treatment-response pilot;
3. SLP review of report V2 and recorder workflow;
4. partner conversations.

Then decide whether the first prospective study should be:

- adult aphasia state-report usability;
- child/DLD recorder feasibility;
- stuttering recovery sample collection;
- or a treatment-response pilot with a partner lab.
